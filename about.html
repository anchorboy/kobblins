About Kobblins

Kobblins are tiny painted rock creatures scattered throughout the world, waiting to be found by curious wanderers. Each Kobblin is unique — hand‑painted, numbered, and part of a themed collection. When someone discovers one, they can scan the QR code on its back to reveal its story, name, and place in the collection.

The idea behind Kobblins is simple: create small moments of delight. Whether tucked under a park bench, hidden near a hiking trail, or resting quietly in a garden, each Kobblin is a surprise meant to brighten someone’s day.

As the project grows, new collections will appear with their own themes, personalities, and lore. Some Kobblins are shy, some mischievous, some adventurous — but all of them are part of a larger world built around exploration, creativity, and community.

Keep your eyes open. You never know when a Kobblin might be watching.
